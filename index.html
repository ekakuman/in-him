Chapter 1 · He was pictured

Shadows that point to Him — and the Three Offices

క్రీస్తు పూర్వరూపాలు — ప్రవక్త, యాజకుడు, రాజు
And beginning at Moses and all the prophets, he expounded unto them in all the scriptures the things concerning himself. Luke 24:27

Last month we traced the types: persons, events and objects the New Testament itself says were shadows of Christ (Col 2:17; Heb 10:1). This study steps back and asks a bigger question. Every one of those shadows was serving one of three anointed offices in Israel: the prophet, the priest and the king. Christ means “the Anointed”, and He fills all three at once.

Messiah · Christ · the Anointed · అభిషిక్తుడు

Jesus Christ — Prophet, Priest & King

Prophets, priests and kings were each anointed with oil (1 Ki 19:16; Exod 29:7; 1 Sam 16:13). God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power (Acts 10:38). The other shadows (Adam, manna, the smitten rock, the bronze serpent, the ark, the sabbath rest) show what He gives through these offices: new headship, life, the Spirit, healing, safety and rest.

Why it had to be Christ

Kept apart in Israel, joined only by God's oath

Judged

Kings who reached for the priest's work

Saul offered the burnt offering himself and lost the kingdom (1 Sam 13:9–14). Uzziah went in to burn incense and was struck with leprosy: It appertaineth not unto thee (2 Chr 26:18).

The offices were not to be combined by human hands.

Approved

The two pictures where God joins them

Melchizedek, king of Salem and priest of the most high God (Gen 14:18): Thou art a priest for ever after the order of Melchizedek (Ps 110:4; Heb 7:1–3). The Branch: he shall be a priest upon his throne (Zech 6:13).

What no man could take, God gave to His Son by oath (Heb 7:20–21).

Past · present · future

How Christ carries the three offices through time

He came · past

Prophet on earth

He preached the kingdom and revealed the Father: he that hath seen me hath seen the Father (John 14:9).

He lives · present

Priest in heaven

Entered into heaven itself, now to appear in the presence of God for us (Heb 9:24); He intercedes now (Rom 8:34).

He comes · future

King over all

The kingdoms of this world are become the kingdoms of our Lord, and of his Christ (Rev 11:15).

This is a matter of emphasis, not separation. He is all three at once: He reigns now at the right hand (Eph 1:20–22), and He still speaks through His word (Heb 12:25).

Why does every shadow lead to one Person? Because God purposed it before the world began.

The Eternal Purpose: All Things Gathered in Christ

దేవుని నిత్య సంకల్పము — సమస్తమును క్రీస్తునందు
That in the dispensation of the fulness of times he might gather together in one all things in Christ, both which are in heaven, and which are on earth; even in him. Ephesians 1:10

The types were not scattered hints. They were pieces of one plan, the eternal purpose which he purposed in Christ Jesus our Lord (Eph 3:11), set before time and carried out in time. Christ is not one point along that line. He is the one in whom the whole line holds together (Col 1:17).

The storyline

From before the foundation to the fulness of times

Tap a station. The upper band is the unseen, heavenly realm; the lower band is earth and history. The gold axis is Christ, through whom both are joined.

Chapter 3 · We live it

On Earth, with a Heavenly Perspective

భూమిపై జీవితం — నిత్య దృష్టితో
While we look not at the things which are seen, but at the things which are not seen: for the things which are seen are temporal; but the things which are not seen are eternal. 2 Corinthians 4:18

Living with a spiritual perspective does not mean looking away from earth. Ordinary earthly life is where the invisible plan becomes visible. Ephesians is built this way: three chapters of what is true in the heavenlies, then three chapters of how we walk on earth.

Ephesians 1–3 · Seated

What is true in heaven

  • Chosen before the foundation (1:4)
  • Blessed with every spiritual blessing (1:3)
  • Seated together in heavenly places (2:6)
  • The church displays God's wisdom to the powers (3:10)

Eph 4:1

“walk worthy of the vocation wherewith ye are called”

Ephesians 4–6 · Walk & Stand

How we live on earth

  • Unity, humility and truthful speech (4:1–32)
  • Marriage, children and parents (5:22–6:4)
  • Work and masters (6:5–9)
  • The armour and the unseen battle (6:10–18)
Anointed in the Anointed One

We share His three offices in daily life

Ye have an unction from the Holy One (1 John 2:20). He hath made us kings and priests unto God and his Father (Rev 1:6).

Prophetic

We speak for Him

That ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you (1 Pet 2:9)
  • Speak the truth in love (Eph 4:15, 25)
  • Give a reason for the hope in you (1 Pet 3:15)
  • Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly (Col 3:16)
Priestly

We come to God for others

To offer up spiritual sacrifices, acceptable to God by Jesus Christ (1 Pet 2:5)
  • Present your bodies a living sacrifice (Rom 12:1)
  • The sacrifice of praise, and doing good (Heb 13:15–16)
  • Intercede for all men (1 Tim 2:1)
Kingly

We rule under Him

Shall reign in life by one, Jesus Christ (Rom 5:17)
  • Rule your own spirit (Prov 16:32)
  • Be faithful over a few things (Matt 25:21)
  • If we suffer, we shall also reign with him (2 Tim 2:12)
We share in His offices; we never replace them. There is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus (1 Tim 2:5).
